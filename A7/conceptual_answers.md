## 1.	Why is the difference between found and designed data?

Found data is data that is already prexisting, not made specifically for the purpose of a specific data science project. It is data you "find". Designed data is data made specifically to answer some question.

## 2.	What are the two primary challenges that necessitate sampling when collecting data?

1. Sampling may be needed if the target population is too large to collect information about. 

2. Sampling may be needed if it is very difficult or expensive to gather information about the entire target population. 


## 3.	Why are website owners more likely to be upset about collecting data using scrapers than using APIs?

APIs are designed for people to gather data from. They have ratelimits in place so that they cannot be abused, and they decide what data users can collect. Website owners don't intend for their websites to be scraped in the first place. They don't have control over who and how often people scrape their site.
